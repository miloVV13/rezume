clepper — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how clepper (the “App”) handles information when you use it. clepper is designed as a local desktop tool: most processing happens on your own computer.

1. Information processed by the App

The App may process:

  • video and audio files you select or download;
  • transcripts, timestamps, subtitles, clips and thumbnails generated from those files;
  • app settings, cache files and logs stored on your computer;
  • if you connect TikTok: an access token, refresh token, TikTok open ID and basic account information returned by TikTok;
  • if you enable a language-model feature: excerpts of transcripts sent to the provider you configure.

2. Local storage

By default, video files, clips, subtitles, manifests and transcripts are stored in folders you choose. Settings and caches are stored in your user profile, typically under ~/.config/clepper and ~/.cache/clepper. TikTok tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions. You can delete these files at any time.

3. TikTok integration

When you connect a TikTok account, you authorize the App to use TikTok’s API. To upload a video, the App sends the selected video file, caption and privacy settings to TikTok. TikTok processes that data under its own privacy policy. The App does not receive your TikTok password. You can revoke access in your TikTok account settings and delete the locally stored tokens.

4. YouTube and other downloads

If you ask the App to download a video from YouTube or another supported site, the URL and any cookies file you provide are used by the downloader on your computer. The video is saved locally. Third-party sites and services process requests under their own policies.

5. Optional language-model features

If you enable LLM-based moment selection, transcript excerpts and related metadata are sent to the OpenAI-compatible provider you configure. This is disabled by default. Do not enable it if you do not want transcript text sent to that provider.

6. Sharing

The App does not sell personal data and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. Data is shared only with services you explicitly use, such as TikTok for uploads or a language-model provider for optional selection. The App’s website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect standard web-server logs under GitHub’s policies.

7. Retention

Local files remain on your computer until you delete them. Tokens remain until you log out, revoke access or delete the token file. The developer does not operate a server that stores your videos or transcripts.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep local credentials and files protected, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your computer, account and network.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children. Do not use it to process or publish content involving children without the required permissions and compliance with applicable law and platform rules.

10. Your choices

You can avoid TikTok integration, avoid optional LLM features, and delete local files and tokens at any time. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights regarding personal data; contact the developer using the details below.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: your-email@example.com or open an issue on GitHub.
